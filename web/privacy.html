Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information Hangul Bricks (“we”) handles when you use our game at hangulquest-phi.vercel.app and related apps. Short version: you can play without an account, your learning progress stays on your device, and we never see your card details.

1. Information stored on your device

Your learning progress (completed levels, mistakes, review schedule, streaks, codex unlocks and a cached subscription status) is saved in your browser’s local storage, or in app storage on mobile. It is not sent to our servers. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

2. Purchases

Subscriptions are sold through Lemon Squeezy, our Merchant of Record. When you buy, Lemon Squeezy collects your name, email address, billing country and payment details to process the payment, calculate taxes and send receipts. We receive order information from Lemon Squeezy (such as your email, plan, order and subscription status) so that we can provide Premium and customer support. We never receive or store your full card number. See the Lemon Squeezy Privacy Policy.

After a purchase, an order reference is stored on your device so that Premium can be restored. When you restore or open the app, this reference is sent to our server only to check with Lemon Squeezy whether your subscription is active.

If you use “Restore with email” on a new device, the email address you enter is sent to our server, which looks up your purchase with Lemon Squeezy. If a purchase is found, a one-time code is sent to that address through our email provider, Resend. We do not store the address; Resend keeps delivery logs under its own retention policy (see the Resend Privacy Policy).

3. Microphone and speech

The web version does not use your microphone. In our iOS app, the optional pronunciation challenge uses on-device speech recognition; recordings are not stored or uploaded by us. You can deny microphone access and still use every other feature.

4. Hosting and technical data

The website is hosted by Vercel. Like most web hosts, Vercel processes technical data such as IP address, browser type and request logs to deliver and secure the site. We do not use advertising trackers and we do not sell or share your personal information for advertising.

5. Emails

If you contact us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you and keep a record of the support request.

6. How long we keep information

Order and billing records are kept for as long as required by tax and accounting law (generally up to 5 years). Support emails are kept for up to 3 years. On-device data stays until you delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object or complain to a supervisory authority. To make a request, email us; we will respond within 30 days.

8. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. International transfers

Our service providers (Lemon Squeezy, Stripe, Vercel and Resend) may process data in the United States and other countries, using appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

10. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date.
