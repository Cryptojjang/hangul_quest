How it works

Stage 1 · Jamo Hunt
Hear a sound, tap the matching letter. Learn all 40 basic and compound consonants and vowels. Free.
Stage 2 · Letter Builder
Drag initial, medial and final blocks into place and watch the syllable come alive. The first 5 levels are free.
Stage 3 · Word Rush
Read real Korean words and match them to pictures against the clock. Premium.
Smart Review
Spaced-repetition review that brings back exactly the sounds you get wrong. 1 free session a day, unlimited with Premium.

Also included for everyone: the Jamo Codex (sound, stroke order and example words for every letter), 60-second mini-lessons, daily challenges and streaks.
